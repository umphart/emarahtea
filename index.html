About Our Company

The Company

EMARAH INVESTMENT LTD is a limited liability company whose registered address is at Dawakin Dakata opposite Idi Customs Poultry Farm, Kano State, Nigeria. Established in the year 2022, we were early to recognize the opportunity for the production of tea flavors and other matters ancillary to produces, distributes, and retails.

Our Mission

• Provide high quality and affordable products
• Create and produce the products that are beneficial to the people
• Respond immediately to the changing need of our customers
• Achieve complete customer satisfaction
• Improve our service continuously

Our Vision

The vision at EMARAH INVESTMENT LTD is of a highly consumable product that is beneficial to its customers, and ready to make things that are useful, with quick response to customers' needs and concerns.

Experience

Our Company has extensive experience in making tea flavors and other matters ancillary to it, such as Hibiscus Tea, Moringa Tea, Black Tea, and Mango Tea on natural sub-herbal tea. We manufacture our products with natural substances without using anything that causes side effects.

Products Cycles

Our company's products circulate across the majority of states in the Northern part of the country, which has gained the recognition of our customers and consumers of our product.
